The old way

Paper tokens weren't built for a busy OPD

Most hospitals still run queues on paper slips, a shouted name, or a WhatsApp guess. It works — until the waiting room fills up.

Reception

No visibility into doctor pace

Reception hands out tokens without knowing how far behind (or ahead) a doctor is running — so every patient asks "how long more?"

Doctor

Interruptions break the rhythm

Doctors lose minutes per patient to staff walking in with the next file, or checking who's actually outside the door.

Patient

Waiting rooms overcrowd

Without a real wait estimate, every patient arrives early and stays put — filling benches that could stay empty until it's their turn.
