Security & data

Your hospital’s queue stays your hospital’s queue

We do not put other hospitals’ logos on this page. We also do not mix hospitals’ data. Isolation is enforced in the database, not only in the interface.

Hospital-by-hospital isolation

Patients, doctors and tokens for Hospital A cannot be read as Hospital B — including by accident in a shared login mistake.

Role-based views

Reception, doctor, manager and patient each receive only the surface their job requires.

Server-checked PINs

Staff PINs are verified on the server. They are not stored as a sticky note inside the browser app.

Encrypted in transit

Every session runs over HTTPS. Data is encrypted on the wire from the screen to the server.

Built on PostgreSQL with Row Level Security. Isolation is a database rule, not a front-end promise. Full write-up on the security page. We do not claim ISO, HIPAA or DPDP certification we do not hold. The product is designed so Indian hospitals can evaluate data handling before a rollout.

Before you involve IT

Questions a careful hospital actually asks

Do we have to replace our HIS or billing software?

No. Smart Queue is the waiting-room layer. You can start with registration and tokens only. Integration is a later conversation, not a precondition.

Where does patient data sit?

Each hospital is isolated at the database layer. We store what the queue needs — identity for the token, doctor assignment, timestamps. Details are on the security page.

What if the internet drops?

The product is live-cloud. A brief drop pauses live updates; reception can continue issuing on reconnection. For a floor that cannot accept that, say so on the demo — we will be direct about fit.

Will patients need an app?

No. Patient view is a browser link. They sign in with the hospital code and the mobile number reception used when booking them — nothing to download.

Can we see it before we pay?

Yes. The four product links above are the real screens. A demo walks your own OPD flow — typically 15 minutes — with no commitment.

Who supports us after go-live?

You write to hello@smarthospitalqueue.com or WhatsApp the same team that sets you up. Early hospitals are not handed to an anonymous ticket queue.
